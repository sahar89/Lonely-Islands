# The Space Between Us

An interactive, generative pixel-art world of floating islands about friendship, separation, migration, belonging, and how hard it is to stay close to one another. Each island is drawn in white and electric blue `#2200FF` with ordered dithering, and each has a single inhabitant going about their day. Between the islands, real human writing drifts through the empty space like slow water.

## Interact
- **Drag empty space** to explore the infinite canvas.
- **Drag an island** to move it. Islands never overlap. The writing parts around it as it moves and closes again behind it.
- **Scroll or pinch** to zoom around the cursor.
- **Sound.** Moving an island makes quiet, synthesised sounds: a soft note as you pick it up, air that follows its speed, a lower note as it settles, and a faint knock when it nudges a neighbour. Press **M** to mute.


## The writing
The collection holds 29 words, 61 sentences and 84 passages, all in the public domain. Most of it is in English, from 29 books first published before 1929: Willa Cather, James Joyce, Edith Wharton, Virginia Woolf, Katherine Mansfield, Anton Chekhov (tr. Constance Garnett), Joseph Conrad, Jane Austen, Charlotte Brontë, Henry David Thoreau, John Keats (letters), Walt Whitman, Emily Dickinson and Rabindranath Tagore. About a third is in its original French, German, Spanish, Italian, Galician and Persian. The Persian comes from [Ganjoor](https://ganjoor.net), classical poets only: Rudaki, Rumi, Saadi, Hafez and Baba Taher.
